# Bookflix Beta Testing Guide

Welcome to the Bookflix beta test! This guide contains specific tasks we'd like you to perform and questions to help us improve the platform before the official launch.

---

## 📋 Tester Tasks

Please try to complete the following actions on the site and take note of anything that feels slow, confusing, or broken.

### 1. User Authentication
- [ ] Create a new account using your email.
- [ ] Log out and log back in.
- [ ] Check if your session stays active after closing the browser tab.

### 2. Browsing & Discovery
- [ ] Browse the homepage and scroll through different book categories.
- [ ] Use the search bar to find a specific book title or author.
- [ ] Apply filters (Genre, Availability) and see if the results update correctly.
- [ ] Open a book's detail page and check if all information (title, description, cover) looks correct.

### 3. The Rental Flow (Critical)
- [ ] Click the "Request to Rent" button on a book page.
- [ ] Fill out the rental form (Name, Phone, Address).
- [ ] Agree to the terms and submit the request.
- [ ] Verify that a "Rent request sent!" message appears.
- [ ] Check if the site correctly handles books that are already "Out of Stock."

### 4. My Rentals Section
- [ ] Navigate to the "My Rentals" page from the menu.
- [ ] Verify that your pending request appears in the list with the correct book details.
- [ ] Check if the status badge correctly shows "⏳ Pending."
- [ ] (Optional) If an admin has marked a book as returned, check if you can use the "⭐ Rate Book" button.

### 5. Suggestion Section
- [ ] Navigate to the "Suggest" page from the menu.
- [ ] Submit a new book suggestion (Title, Author, Genre).
- [ ] Verify that your suggestion appears in the "Your Past Suggestions" list immediately.
- [ ] Check if the status badge correctly shows "Pending."

### 6. Mobile & Performance
- [ ] Open the site on your mobile phone's browser.
- [ ] Try to navigate the menu and scroll through books using touch.
- [ ] Test the "Add to Home Screen" (PWA) feature if available.

---

## ❓ Feedback Questions

After you've explored the site, please answer these questions:

### General Experience
1. **First Impression**: What was your first thought when the page loaded?
2. **Speed**: Did the site feel fast? Were there any pages that took too long to load?
3. **Clarity**: Was it clear how to rent a book, or did you have to search for the button?

### Visuals & UI
4. **Design**: Are there any parts of the site that look "broken" or unpolished on your screen size?
5. **Readability**: Is the text easy to read? Are the colors comfortable for your eyes?

### Bugs & Issues
6. **Errors**: Did you encounter any "Page Not Found" (404) or "Application Error" screens?
7. **Broken Links**: Did you click any buttons or links that did nothing?

### Suggestions
8. **Missing Features**: Is there anything you expected to see (e.g., "Favorites," "Reviews") that is currently missing?
9. **Final Thought**: On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to use this to rent books?

---

> [!TIP]
> **Reporting Bugs**: If you find a bug, please take a screenshot and note down what device (iPhone, Android, PC) and browser (Chrome, Safari) you were using!
